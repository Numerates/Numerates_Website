Our Story

About Numerates

MIT-WPU's student-led math and tech club — building a community where curiosity, collaboration and problem-solving come together.

Who We Are

Where Math
Meets Community.

Numerates started with a simple idea — that math and technology shouldn't feel intimidating. We're a student-run club at MIT-WPU that creates spaces for curiosity, collaboration and real-world problem solving.

From data science bootcamps to campus-wide treasure hunts, we run events that push you just past your comfort zone. Every semester, we bring students together to learn, compete and grow.

Whether you're a math enthusiast, a tech geek, or just curious — there's a place for you at Numerates.

Our Purpose

What Drives Numerates

🎯

Mission

To create an inclusive space where students from all backgrounds can explore mathematics and technology through hands-on workshops, competitions and collaborative projects.

🔭

Vision

To be MIT-WPU's go-to community for students who want to apply mathematical thinking to solve real-world problems and build meaningful careers in tech.

📋

Objectives

Run regular workshops and events. Build a strong alumni network. Foster interdisciplinary learning. Create opportunities to apply math in real contexts.
